Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer towing 24/7 in Rhode Island?
Yes — Centri Auto Towing & Mechanical dispatches 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including holidays. Call (401) 420-9069 any time.
What areas do you serve?
We're based in West Warwick and regularly serve Narragansett, Warwick, North Kingstown, South Kingstown, Exeter, East Greenwich, Westerly, Charlestown, Hopkinton, and Richmond, RI. Give us a call if you're outside this list — we may still be able to help.
What kinds of vehicles can you tow?
We handle light and medium-duty vehicles — cars, SUVs, and trucks — using flatbed and wrecker equipment.
Do you offer roadside help besides towing?
Yes. Alongside towing, we offer battery jump starts, battery testing and replacement, fuel delivery, winching & recovery, tire changes, and lockout service.
How much will my tow or service cost?
Pricing depends on your location, vehicle, and the service needed. Call us at (401) 420-9069 and we'll give you a clear quote up front before dispatching.
Do I need to be there when the truck arrives?
In most cases, yes — we typically need the vehicle owner or an authorized driver present. Let us know when you call if that's not possible and we'll walk you through the options.
